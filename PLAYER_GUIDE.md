# Seasonal SMP — Player Guide

## Bounds
On first join, you are automatically assigned a **Bound** — your elemental affinity. Your bound is permanent and defines your passive effects, season interactions, and abilities.

| Bound | Element | Passive Effect | Season Bonus | Season Penalty |
|-------|---------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Spring** | Bloom | Regeneration I | Spring: Regen + Speed | Other seasons: Hunger |
| **Summer** | Solar | Fire Resistance I | Summer: Fire Res + Strength | Other seasons: Slowness |
| **Autumn** | Harvest | Luck I | Autumn: Haste | Other seasons: Slowness |
| **Winter** | Frost | Resistance I | Winter: Resistance | Other seasons: Hunger |

- View your bound with `/bound`
- Your bound determines which **bound sword** you can use
- Your bound determines your **bound ability**

---

## Commands

### `/trust`
Manage trusted players. Trusted players **cannot damage you** in PvP — attacks between trusted players are cancelled.
- `/trust add <player>` — Trust a player
- `/trust remove <player>` — Remove trust
- `/trust list` — List your trusted allies

### `/bound`
View bound information.
- `/bound` — Show your current bound
- `/bound view <player>` — View another player's bound
- `/bound help` — Show help

### `/ability`
Use your bound's special ability. Has a cooldown.

| Bound | Ability | Effect |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Spring** | Bloom | Heals you, removes negative potion effects, and heals nearby trusted allies |
| **Summer** | Solar Burst | Deals damage in an area around you, sets enemies on fire, and knocks them back |
| **Autumn** | Harvest | Breaks fully grown crops in an area around you |
| **Winter** | Frozen Heart | Grants you Resistance, slows nearby enemies, and gives you a health boost |

## Swords

### Bound Swords
Each bound has a unique **Legendary Sword**. These swords are **unbreakable** and have powerful enchantments.
- **Right-click** with your bound sword to activate your **sword ability**
- Each bound sword has a unique AoE ability that affects nearby enemies

| Bound | Sword Ability |
|-------|--------------|
| **Spring** | Slows and weakens nearby enemies |
| **Summer** | Deals heavy AoE damage and sets enemies on fire |
| **Autumn** | Instantly defeats enemies with low health |
| **Winter** | Freezes nearby enemies with Slowness, Mining Fatigue, and Weakness |

### Seasonal Blade
A legendary weapon forged from the power of all four seasons. Right-clicking activates a **season-dependent ability**:
- **Spring** — Pushes enemies back and slows them
- **Summer** — Calls down a solar judgment at your target, dealing heavy damage and burning enemies
- **Autumn** — Launches you forward with a gust of wind
- **Winter** — Freezes your target area with ice, damaging and slowing all enemies

The Seasonal Blade also has passive effects that trigger randomly while held.

## Seasons
The world cycles through **4 seasons** every **2 hours**. Each season changes weather, world behavior, and how your bound interacts with the world.

| Season | Weather | World Effect |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Spring** | More rain and thunder | Faster crop growth, flower particles, bees are passive |
| **Summer** | Heat, fire particles, thunderstorms | More hostile mobs, longer days, fire particles |
| **Autumn** | Rain, falling leaves, fog | Leaf decay, extra loot from mobs |
| **Winter** | Snow, longer nights | Water freezes, ice spikes, fewer passive mobs, longer nights |

- A boss bar at the top of your screen always shows the current season
- Season transitions are announced server-wide with a sound and particle effect
- Your bound's passive effects change based on the current season (see Bounds table above)
- In Summer, Summer players melt nearby ice and snow just by being near it

## Combat Rules
These restrictions are **always enforced** when in combat:

| Category | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Enchantments | Sharpness max level 4, Protection max level 3 |
| Items | Max 1 Totem of Undying, Max 1 Wolf |
| Weapons | Tridents, Crossbows, and Bows with Punch are blocked |
| Explosives | TNT, TNT minecarts, End Crystals, Respawn Anchors, Bed bombs are blocked |
| Movement | No Ender Pearls, no extended water running |
| Mining | Netherite Pickaxe only |
| Combat Logging | Quitting during combat is broadcast to the server |

Combat lasts **15 seconds** after hitting or being hit.

## General
- The server is **whitelisted** — only approved players can join.
- Bound swords are **unbreakable**.
- Trust is the only way to avoid accidental PvP with teammates.
- Your bound is assigned on first join and cannot be changed by players.
